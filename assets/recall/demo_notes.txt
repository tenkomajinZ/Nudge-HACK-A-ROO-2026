NUDGE — demonstration notes (page 1)

These notes support the bundled simulated quiz. Uploaded files are NOT analyzed in simulated mode.

Retrieval practice means bringing information to mind without looking at the source.
Spacing distributes practice across separate study periods.
Feedback identifies mistakes so the learner can correct them.
Interleaving mixes different kinds of problems within a practice session.
Elaboration connects new information to what the learner already knows.
Self-explanation describes why a solution step makes sense.
Silencing optional notifications reduces interruptions during focus time.
A planned break separates focus blocks and allows a pause before returning.
Missed concepts are useful targets for the next focused review.
A quiz samples understanding; a perfect score does not prove complete mastery of every concept.
